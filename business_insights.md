# Key Business Insights

## KPIs

- Total Sales: $2.30M
- Total Profit: $286.4K
- Profit Margin: 12.5%
- Total Orders: 5,009
- Total Quantity: 37,871
- Average Order Value: $458.56
- Average Profit per Order: $57.18
- Top Category (Sales): Technology
- Most Profitable Category: Technology
- Best Performing Region (Profit): West

## What the data tells us

- Technology is the most profitable category, generating $145.5K profit on $836.2K sales (17.4% margin).
- Furniture is the weakest-margin category at 2.5%, despite $741.7K in sales.
- Tables is a profit-draining sub-category: $207.0K sales produced -$17.7K profit (-8.6% margin).
- Loss-making sub-categories overall: Tables, Bookcases, Supplies.
- West is the strongest region by profit ($108.4K, 14.9% margin), while Central has the weakest regional margin at 7.9% on $501.2K sales.
- Sales peaked in 2017-11 ($118.4K) and were lowest in 2014-02 ($4.5K). Fall is the strongest season by sales.
- The highest monthly profit occurred in 2016-12 ($17.9K); the weakest profit month was 2015-01 (-$3.3K). Highest monthly margin: 2016-10 (27.2%).
- Discounting is a material profit risk: the High (>40%) band delivered -77.4% margin versus 29.5% in the No Discount band.
- Discount is negatively correlated with profit (r = -0.22), while sales and profit move together more weakly (r = 0.48).
- Annual sales moved from $484.0K in 2014 to $733.2K in 2017, with profit margin 10.2% vs 12.7%.
- 301 products are net loss-making over the period and should be treated as portfolio risk, not merely low-volume SKUs.

## Business recommendations

### Product portfolio optimization
- Finding: Sub-categories currently destroying profit: Tables, Bookcases, Supplies.
- Action: Reprice, bundle, or delist chronic loss-makers after reviewing contribution to customer baskets. Do not expand inventory in these families until margin is positive.

### Discount optimization
- Finding: High (>40%) transactions produced -77.4% margin on $128.6K sales.
- Action: Cap deep discounts on already-negative-margin SKUs. Require a margin floor before promotions above 20%, especially in Furniture.

### Pricing optimization
- Finding: 194 products combine average discount ≥ 20% with negative profit.
- Action: Review list price and promotional eligibility for these SKUs. High-revenue, low-margin items should be repriced before further discounting.

### Inventory optimization
- Finding: 873 products sit at or below median quantity and order frequency (slow-moving candidates).
- Action: Reduce replenishment on slow movers that are also low-profit. Keep inventory on high-sales/high-profit products that also move quickly.

### Regional strategy
- Finding: Central has the weakest margin (7.9%); West has the strongest (14.9%).
- Action: Audit mix and discounting in the weakest-margin region. Replicate assortment and pricing discipline from the strongest-margin region.

### Seasonal planning
- Finding: Demand and profit concentrate toward Fall; the weakest sales month in the series is 2014-02.
- Action: Align working capital and promotions with the peak season rather than pushing deep discounts in already-weak months if those months also show poor margins.

### Customer segment strategy
- Finding: Consumer contributes the most profit ($134.1K on $1.16M, 11.5% margin). Consumer has the weakest segment margin at 11.5%.
- Action: Protect Consumer share in Technology. Improve mix and discount discipline for Consumer rather than chasing volume alone.

### Risk control
- Finding: 301 products are net unprofitable.
- Action: Create a monthly 'profit leak' watchlist of the worst 25 SKUs and assign owners for price, discount, or discontinuation decisions.


## Performance matrix meaning

- **A. High Sales + High Profit:** Core engines of the business. Protect availability, avoid unnecessary discounting, and invest in inventory and visibility.
- **B. High Sales + Low Profit:** Revenue-rich but economically weak. These SKUs inflate the top line while consuming working capital. Immediate pricing, discount, and cost review.
- **C. Low Sales + High Profit:** Hidden margin gems. Consider targeted marketing, better placement, or bundling to grow volume without giving away margin.
- **D. Low Sales + Low Profit:** Candidates for rationalization. If they are also slow-moving, reduce stock or exit unless they serve a strategic basket role.
