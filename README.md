Retail Business Performance \& Profitability Analysis



Project Overview



This project analyzes retail business performance to uncover key drivers of sales and profitability. The analysis focuses on sales, profit, products, categories, sub-categories, regions, segments, discounts, and seasonal performance.



The project combines Python, SQL, and Power BI to transform raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights through data cleaning, exploratory analysis, business analysis, and interactive dashboarding.



Objectives



\- Analyze overall sales and profit performance

\- Identify high-performing and low-performing products

\- Analyze regional, category, sub-category, and segment performance

\- Study monthly and seasonal sales trends

\- Examine the relationship between discount and profit

\- Identify profitability patterns and margin opportunities

\- Generate business insights to support better decision-making
